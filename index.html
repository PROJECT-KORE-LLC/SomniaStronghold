🛠️ WORKSHOP MANUAL

Your device is a private workshop. This vault enforces that philosophy by isolating all calculations and data strictly within your browser's local instance.

  • Global Snapshot Tool: Located in the top navigation bar. Clicking 📸 Snapshot will immediately tally the cumulative volume of your active ledger, drop a timestamped/dated journal entry into the Almanac Breadcrumbs, and trigger a raw JSON download of your financial data to your hard drive.
  • Strict Separation of Data: The Vault Tab purely imports/exports aesthetic Vibes (Colors, Particles, Audio layouts). To import or export your raw financial data, you must use the isolated Ledger Data Backup tools found inside The Stronghold side panel.
  • Dynamic Pie Charts: The Stronghold generates CSS conic-gradient pie charts by executing inline math based exclusively on the entries visible in your active tab filter. Blank context = Blank chart.
  • Interactive Almanac: The Almanac Calendar provides a cumulative view. Household bills are Blue, Business are Gold, Caretaker are Purple. Click on any colored day block to open that day's specific bills in the side panel to mark them Paid/Unpaid.
  • Abacus Keyboard Support: Open the Abacus. You can type natively on your keyboard NumPad. Hit Enter to solve, or C / Escape / Delete to clear the board.

🤖 THE KEEPER (AI SANDBOX)

1. Navigate to aistudio.google.com and generate an API key.
2. Open The Keeper tab from the top nav.
3. Paste your key and click Save. The key is securely written to your local profile cache and never leaves your machine.
4. Command The Keeper to parse loose text into strict JSON arrays that auto-populate the active Stronghold form.
